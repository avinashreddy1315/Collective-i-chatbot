Q: How do I reset my password?
A: If you’ve forgotten your password, click “Forgot Password” on the login page, enter your registered email, then follow the reset link we send you. Be sure your new password is at least 8 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols.

Q: Can I change my account email?
A: Yes. Go to Account Settings → Personal Info, click “Edit” next to your email address, enter the new email, then confirm via the verification link we send.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: We’re sorry to see you go! Submit a support request with the subject “Delete Account” and include your username. Once verified, we’ll permanently remove your data within 7 days.
